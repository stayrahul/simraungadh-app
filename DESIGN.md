# Simraungadh Civic Modern Theme

## Concept
The design should feel official yet highly approachable and modern. We want to blend trust (deep blues, clean typography) with community engagement (vibrant accents, soft rounded corners, glassmorphism).

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Indigo):** `#4F46E5` - Used for primary actions, active states, and brand highlights.
- **Secondary (Emerald):** `#10B981` - Used for verified badges, successful actions, and official status.
- **Backgrounds:** `#F8FAFC` for light mode, `#0B0F19` for dark mode.
- **Cards:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` with a very subtle `#E2E8F0` border for definition.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans - Bold, tight letter spacing for a punchy, modern feel.
- **Body:** Inter - Highly legible, neutral, and clean.

## UI Elements
- **Cards:** Large border radii (`24px` to `32px`). Soft, expansive drop shadows (`0 8px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`).
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation bars and sticky headers should have a blurred, translucent effect (`backdrop-blur-xl bg-white/70`).
- **Buttons:** Fully rounded (pill shape) or large `16px` rounded rectangles for primary actions. They should feel substantial.
- **Spacing:** Generous padding. Elements should feel breathable. Use `16px` or `24px` gaps between major components.

## Tone
Professional, optimistic, engaging, and highly scannable.
